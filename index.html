StudiPulse — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 12, 2026  •  Last Updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how The Penelope Group LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use StudiPulse ("the App"). StudiPulse is an independent, fan-made wait-time app for Universal Orlando Resort and is not affiliated with Universal, Comcast NBCUniversal, or any third-party IP holder featured in the parks — see our Terms of Service for details.

We do not use any advertising or analytics SDK, and we do not collect the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA). No App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown because we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the App's core features (wait times, alerts, itinerary planning, park maps)
  • To authenticate your account and sync your data across your devices
  • To send push notifications you've explicitly opted into
  • To process subscription purchases through Apple's App Store and our billing provider, RevenueCat
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse (e.g., rate-limiting alert creation)

3. Location Data in Detail

Location data is processed primarily on your device. Background location (used to auto-detect when you've finished a ride, so your itinerary updates itself, and to send a "leaving the resort" notification) requires your explicit permission and can be revoked at any time in your device's Settings. We do not sell location data to third parties or use it for advertising.

4. Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Functions) — Firebase Privacy & Security
  • RevenueCat (subscription management) — RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Expo (push notification delivery) — Expo Privacy Policy
  • ThemeParks.wiki API (third-party wait-time data provider) — no personal data is shared with this service; it is used only to fetch public wait-time information
  • Apple App Store / Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In — governed by Apple's and Google's own privacy policies

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account at any time from the Profile section of the App, which removes your account data from our systems. Some anonymized, aggregated data (e.g., wait-time history used to improve predictions) is not tied to your account and is retained separately.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data. You can exercise most of these rights directly in the App (Profile → Account settings) or by contacting us at the email below.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document and, where appropriate, through an in-app notice.
