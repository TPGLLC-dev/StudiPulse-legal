Frequently Asked Questions

  • Wait times look stale or wrong. Pull down on the park screen to refresh, and confirm you have a network connection. Live data refreshes automatically every 2 minutes.
  • I'm not getting wait-time or geofence alerts. Check Settings → Notifications on your device and confirm Location permission is set to "Always" for background geofence alerts.
  • How do I cancel my subscription? Manage or cancel any subscription directly in your device's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions — StudiPulse does not process payments directly.
  • Express Pass wait times aren't showing for a ride. Not every attraction offers Express Pass; the badge only appears for rides where Universal Orlando publishes a live Express line wait.
